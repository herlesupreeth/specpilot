## 4	Background

At 3GPP RAN#97-e meeting, a basket Work Item on “High power UE (power class 1.5 or 2) for intra-band Carrier Aggregation combinations of a single NR FR1 TDD band” [2] was approved for Rel-18. The objectives of the core part are as follows:

This WI will address the following intra-band Carrier Aggregation cases of a single NR FR1 TDD band:

-	with PC2 for UL intra-band CA configuration

-	with PC2 for single uplink carrier

-	with PC1.5 for single uplink carrier

-	with the following objectives:

1) Specify the band-combination specific RF requirements including

a) UE maximum output power and Tx power tolerance

b) A-MPR requirements if needed

2) Define requirements for PC1.5 according to following conditions

a) Include intra-band DL CA band combinations with PC1.5 on the single carrier uplink in supporting of 2x26dBm PA configuration.

Note 0: Other additional requirements due to the high power on UL may be added via WID revisions, if necessary.
