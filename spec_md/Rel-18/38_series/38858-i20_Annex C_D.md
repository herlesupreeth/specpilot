########## Annex &lt;C&gt;:
System level simulation calibration

## C.1	SLS calibration methodology

**Calibration scenarios**

The SLS calibration focuses on the following scenarios of SBFD Deployment Case 1:

-	FR1

-	Urban Macro

-	Indoor office

-	FR2-1

-	Dense Urban Macro layer

-	Indoor office

**Calibration metrics**

The following metrics are considered for SLS calibration:

-	CDF of gNB-UE coupling loss

-	CDF of Inter-gNB coupling loss

-	CDF of Inter-UE coupling loss

-	Optional: CDF of DL SINR for legacy TDD/ DL SINR in DL-only slots for SBFD

-	Optional: CDF of DL SINR in SBFD slots

-	Optional: CDF of UL SINR for legacy TDD/ UL SINR in UL-only slots for SBFD

-	Optional: CDF of UL SINR in SBFD slots
